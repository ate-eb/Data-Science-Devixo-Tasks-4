# Marketing Campaign & Customer Behavior Analytics

An end-to-end business intelligence and analytics solution developed for **Devixo Solutions (Task 04)**[cite: 2, 4]. This project transforms raw customer demographic, spending, and marketing campaign data into an interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboard designed to drive data-backed decision-making[cite: 1, 4].

---

## 📋 Project Overview
Retail and direct-to-consumer organizations frequently struggle with low marketing campaign ROI and untargeted promotional strategies. This project analyzes customer behavior, spending habits across product categories, channel preferences (Web, Store, Catalog, and Deals), and historical campaign responsiveness to optimize marketing effectiveness[cite: 1, 4].

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
* **Data Preparation & Cleaning:** Power Query (M Language)[cite: 4]
* **Data Modeling & DAX Engineering:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop[cite: 1, 4]
* **Visualization & Dashboarding:** Power BI (Fluent 2 UI Theme)[cite: 1]
* **Version Control & Documentation:** Git & GitHub[cite: 2]

---

## 📊 Dashboard Architecture & Power BI Pages
The Power BI report (`task4.pbix`) is structured into **five core analytical pages** with synchronized cross-page filtering and slicers (`Customer_age_group`, `Education`, `Marital_Status`, and `Campaign`)[cite: 1, 5]:

1. **Executive Summary:** High-level KPI cards (`Total Customers`, `Total Spending`, `Total Purchases`, `Average Income`, `Average Spend Per Purchase`, `Campaign Response Rate`), category spending breakdowns, age distribution, and income-vs-spending correlation[cite: 1].
2. **Customer Demographics:** Detailed profiling of customer counts and average income across age cohorts, education levels, and marital statuses[cite: 1].
3. **Spending Analysis:** Category-level expenditure ranking, spending by age groups, and income-to-spending scatter analytics[cite: 1].
4. **Purchase Behaviour:** Omnichannel performance comparison (Store vs. Web vs. Catalog vs. Deals), monthly web traffic vs. web purchases, deal usage distribution, and average purchases per customer[cite: 1].
5. **Campaign Analysis:** Individual campaign acceptance comparison, acceptance/rejection share, recency-to-response correlation, and campaign reach across demographic segments[cite: 1].

---

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & DAX Measures
* **Total Customers:** Distinct count of active customer profiles[cite: 1].
* **Total Spending:** Aggregate monetary expenditure across all product categories[cite: 1].
* **Total Purchases:** Cumulative transaction volume across Store, Web, and Catalog channels[cite: 1].
* **Average Income:** Mean annual household income[cite: 1].
* **Average Spend Per Purchase:** Revenue efficiency per transaction[cite: 1].
* **Web Purchase Rate:** Proportion of total purchases completed via the e-commerce channel[cite: 1].
* **Campaign Response Rate:** Percentage of target audience accepting promotional campaigns[cite: 1].

---

## 📈 Key Analytical Insights
1. **Income Drives Spend:** Strong positive linear correlation between household income and total spending[cite: 1].
2. **Product Skew:** Core discretionary/staple categories (Wines and Meat) generate the vast majority of revenue compared to secondary lines[cite: 1].
3. **Age & Demographics:** Middle-aged and older adult cohorts represent both the largest customer volume and the highest spending power[cite: 1].
4. **Channel Dominance:** Physical retail stores remain the primary transaction channel, while web purchases capture a significant digital share[cite: 1].
5. **Campaign Inefficiencies:** Broad mass-blast campaigns suffer from high rejection rates, whereas recent purchasers (low recency) show much higher conversion propensities[cite: 1].
